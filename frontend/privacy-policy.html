Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what Digital Invites collects when you request a demo, host an invitation with us or reply to an invitation as a guest, and what we do with it.

1. Who we are

Digital Invites ("we", "us") runs the website digital-invite.net. We design, set up and host digital invitations for weddings, engagements, birthdays and other celebrations. Our contact details are at the end of this policy.

There are three kinds of people in this policy. A host is someone who orders an invitation from us. A guest is someone a host invites. A visitor is anyone who uses our website.

For a guest's details, the host decides who is invited and what the invitation asks. We store and handle those details on the host's behalf, to run their invitation.

2. What we collect

From visitors who request a demo

When you fill in the request form on our website we collect your name, the type of event, your email address or phone number, a company name if you give one, and your message. We also record the time and the internet address (IP address) the request came from.

From hosts

  • Account details: your name, your email address or phone number, and a password. We also record when you last signed in.
  • Invitation content: the names on the invitation, the date, the venues and their addresses, the timeline of the day, your wording, and any photos, video or music you give us.
  • Gift details: if you want them shown, the bank or wallet details you ask us to display to your guests. We only display them. We do not take or handle gift payments.
  • Your guest list: each guest's name, how many seats they have, their personal link, and their table and seat if you use seating.
  • Your notification choices: whether you would like to be told about replies.

From guests

When a guest replies to an invitation we collect:

  • whether they are coming, and how many people are in their party;
  • the names of the people in their party;
  • if the invitation asks and the guest answers: an age group, a meal choice and any dietary needs;
  • if the guest gives them: a contact name, email address, phone number and a message for the host;
  • the time of the reply, the IP address it came from and the type of browser used.

Dietary needs can say something about a person's health or beliefs. Guests should only share what they are comfortable sharing with their host.

A guest who looks up their table enters the code from their host. We use it to show their table and seat.

From our own team

We keep a log of the changes our team makes to invitations and accounts, with the time and the IP address.

What we do not collect

We do not take card details or any other payment details through this website. We do not ask guests to create an account. We do not read your phone's contacts: a guest list reaches us only when the host types it in or imports a spreadsheet.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your request and send you a demo and a quote.
  • To build your invitation and show it to anyone who opens its link.
  • To show each guest their own invitation when they open their personal link.
  • To record guests' replies and show them to the host in the host's dashboard.
  • To show guests their table and seat.
  • To keep the service secure, for example by limiting repeated table look-ups and keeping a log of our team's changes.

We do not sell personal details, we do not use them for advertising, and we do not send marketing messages to guests.

Where the law asks for a legal basis, we rely on our agreement with the host (to build and run the invitation), on our legitimate interest in answering requests and keeping the service secure, and on consent where we ask for it.

4. Cookies and analytics

We do not set cookies and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Two small things are stored in your own browser:

  • Sign-in: when a host signs in, the browser keeps a sign-in key until the tab is closed. It expires by itself after about an hour.
  • Invitation details: some invitation designs can read invitation details that were saved earlier in the same browser. They stay on that device.

Some parts of our pages come from other companies, which may see your IP address when your browser loads them. They are listed in the next section.

5. Who can see it

  • The host. A guest's reply is shown to the host of that invitation. That is the purpose of the reply.
  • Anyone with the link. Anyone who has an invitation's link can open the invitation and see what the host chose to show on it, including venues and gift details. A guest's personal link also shows that guest's name. Hosts should share links only with the people they are inviting.
  • Our team. The people who set up and support invitations can see the details they need to do that.

6. How long we keep it

  • Demo requests: up to 12 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a host.
  • An invitation, its guest list, replies, photos and other media: while the invitation is online, which is until three months after the event unless the host agreed a different period with us. When an invitation is deleted, its guest list, replies and media are deleted with it.
  • Host accounts: until the invitation they belong to is deleted.
  • The log of our team's changes: up to 12 months.

You can ask us to delete your details sooner. See "Your rights" below.

7. How we protect it

  • Our pages are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored in a scrambled form that cannot be read back, even by us.
  • A host can only see their own invitation, guest list and replies.
  • A host can replace a guest's personal link at any time, which switches the old one off.
  • Sign-ins expire by themselves, and repeated table look-ups are limited.

No system is perfectly secure. If a problem affects your details, we will tell you, and the authorities where the law requires it.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the details we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your details;
  • stop using your details in a way you object to.

Contact us using the details below. We reply within 30 days. We may ask a question or two to be sure the request comes from you.

Hosts can add, change and remove guests in their dashboard at any time.

Guests can also ask their host directly, because the host manages the guest list. If you write to us, we may pass your request to the host of that invitation.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

9. Children

Our service is for adults who are organising an event. Hosts must be at least 18, and we do not knowingly collect details directly from children.

A host or a guest may give us a child's name, age group or meal needs as part of a guest list or a reply. The adult who gives us those details is responsible for having the right to share them. A parent or guardian can ask us to remove a child's details at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and change the date at the top. If a change matters to how we use your details, we will tell hosts before it takes effect.

11. Contact

For any question about your details, or to use your rights:
