Last updated: 10 November 2026

These terms apply when you request a demo, order or host a digital invitation with Digital Invites, and when you open or reply to an invitation as a guest.

1. About these terms

Digital Invites ("we", "us") runs the website digital-invite.net. We design, set up and host digital invitations for weddings, engagements, birthdays, baby showers, graduations and other celebrations.

In these terms a host is the person who orders an invitation, and a guest is a person the host invites. By requesting a demo, ordering an invitation, signing in to the host dashboard or replying to an invitation, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

2. Our service

A digital invitation is a web page made for one event. It opens from a link on any phone, tablet or computer. Depending on what you order, it can include:

  • your names, wording, date, venues and the timeline of the day;
  • your photos, a video clip and music;
  • a countdown to the day and directions to each venue;
  • a reply form, so guests can accept or decline and tell you who is coming;
  • a personal link for each guest, with their name on the invitation;
  • table seating, with a code guests use to find their table;
  • gift details that you choose to show;
  • a host dashboard where you see replies and manage your guest list.

We set up the invitation for you. You share the link with your guests yourself, for example by WhatsApp, SMS or email. We do not send invitations to your guests, and we do not organise the event.

3. Demos, quotes and orders

  • Demo. When you send a request through our website, we reply with a live sample invitation and a quote. A demo is free and does not commit you to anything.
  • Quote. The quote states the design, the features included and the price. It is valid for 30 days unless it says otherwise.
  • Order. Your order is confirmed when you accept the quote in writing (email or a messaging app counts) and make the payment the quote asks for.
  • What we need from you. The occasion, the date, the venues, the names and your wording. Photos, music and your guest list can follow.
  • Your sample. We set up the invitation and send you a private sample to check. Please read it carefully: names, dates, times, addresses and map locations. You are responsible for the accuracy of the details you approve.
  • Timing. We tell you when to expect your sample once we have your details. Sending us your details late may delay it.

4. Price and payment

  • Work you ask for that is not in the quote, such as a new section or a second design, is quoted separately before we start it.

Gifts. If your invitation shows bank or wallet details for gifts, guests pay you directly. We do not receive, hold or pass on gift money, and we are not responsible for those payments.

5. Changes, cancellation and refunds

  • Changes before you approve. Two rounds of changes to your sample are included. Further rounds may be charged, and we will tell you the cost first.
  • Changes after you share the link. If a detail changes, such as the time or a venue, tell us and we update the invitation. The link your guests already have stays the same. Moving to a different design is a new order.
  • If you cancel before we start. You receive a full refund of anything you have paid.
  • If you cancel after we have started. An invitation is made to order for your event. Once we have begun setting it up, the amount paid covers the work done and is not refunded, unless the law where you live gives you a right to a refund.
  • If your event moves or is cancelled. We update the date or take the invitation offline at no charge. The price is not refunded once the invitation has been delivered.
  • If we cannot deliver. If we are unable to deliver the invitation you ordered, we refund what you paid for it in full.

  • Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what is done through your account. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used it.
  • Guests need no account. A guest opens the invitation and replies from its link.

8. Your guests and their replies

You decide who is on your guest list. By adding a guest you confirm that you may share their name with us for the invitation.

  • Anyone who has your invitation's link can open it and see what you chose to show, including venues and gift details. Share it only with the people you are inviting.
  • A guest's personal link shows that guest's name. If a link reaches the wrong person, you can replace it in your dashboard.
  • Replies are recorded as guests send them. We do not check that a reply is true or that a guest will attend. Please confirm final numbers with your venue and caterer yourself.
  • Guests' replies are for you. Use them only to organise your event.
  • You can export your replies from your dashboard at any time. Please keep your own copy.

Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect from hosts and guests and how long we keep it.
