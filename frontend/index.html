Questions hosts ask

Eight answers about designs, replies, sharing and price.

Can the invitation match our event?

Yes. The names, wording, photos, colours, venues, timeline, music and gift details are yours to set. If you need a section we do not have yet, tell us and we will add it for you.

How do guests reply to the invitation?

Inside the invitation, in one tap. They can say how many are coming, add each guest's name and leave you a message. You see every reply as it arrives.

Will the invitation open on every phone?

Yes. Guests tap the link and the invitation opens on any phone, tablet or computer. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.

What do you need to start our invitation?

The occasion, the date, the venues and the names. Your photos, music and guest list can follow once you have seen the sample.

How do we share the invitation?

You get one link that you can send by WhatsApp, SMS, email or social media. You can also give each guest a link of their own, with their name on the invitation.

Can each guest get their own invitation link?

Yes. Import your guest list, set the seats for each guest, and everyone gets a link with their name on it. Their reply is matched to them.

How much does an invitation cost?

The price depends on the design and the features you need. Send us a few details about your event and we reply with a quote within one business day.

Can we change the invitation after it is sent?

Yes. Tell us what changed and we update the invitation. The link your guests already have stays the same.
